OpenHP1

An open-source reimplementation of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (PC). It runs from your own copy of the original game. Imported files stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

This browser does not provide WebGPU. On iPhone and iPad, use Safari on iOS 26 or later.

Checking for imported game files…

Import the installed game folder (the one containing System, Maps, and Textures). On iPhone, put a ZIP of that folder in the Files app first: long-press the folder and choose Compress, or create the ZIP on a computer. Add this page to your Home Screen to play full screen.
